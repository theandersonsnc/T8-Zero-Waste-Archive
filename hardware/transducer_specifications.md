# Topological Hardware Specifications

Derived from Patent Application 63/947,035 and the Gaia Engine formulations.

## 1. Topological Phononic Transducer
Translates mathematical exact-ratio OAM into macroscopic kinetic phase-shifts.
* **Core:** alpha''-Fe16N2 (Iron-Nitride) for massive, directional magnetic anisotropy without rare-earth dependencies.
* **Filter (The 15th Gate):** Gold nanotubes coated in a self-assembled monolayer of chiral oligopeptides utilizing the Chiral-Induced Spin Selectivity (CISS) effect.
* **Waveguide:** Base-20 icosahedral acoustic metamaterial generating twisted vorticle wavefronts.

## 2. Topological Photonic Memory Core
* **Encoder:** Nonlinear plasmonic metasurface multiplexing into 60 distinct OAM modes.
* **Substrate:** Molybdenum Disulfide (MoS2) over Phase-Change Chalcogenide Glass (Ge2Sb2Te5). Structurally locks topological knots requiring zero continuous power (Anthro-Joules) to maintain data state.
